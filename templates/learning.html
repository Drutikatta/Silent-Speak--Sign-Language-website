    Sign language is about seeing the voices
    rather than hearing them

    A learning page in sign language is like a picture book with instructions on how to do different signs. Each sign is shown with pictures or drawings that explain how to move your hands, face, and body to make the sign correctly. It also tells you what the sign means and when to use it. These pages help people learn sign language step by step, so they can communicate better with others who use sign language.
